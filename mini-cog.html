Step 1 — Word Registration
Three word presentation
1 of 3
Say to patient: "Please listen carefully. I am going to say three words that I want you to repeat back to me now and try to remember. The words are … Please say them for me now."
Attempts:
Note: If unable to repeat words after 3 attempts, proceed to Step 2 (clock drawing) and document this.
🕐
Step 2 — Clock Drawing
Present pre-printed circle to patient
2 of 3

Hand the patient a pre-printed circle. Say:

"Next, I want you to draw a clock for me. First, put in all of the numbers where they go."

When complete, say:

"Now, set the hands to 10 past 11."

Note: You may repeat the instructions — this is not a memory test. If the clock is not complete within 3 minutes, proceed to Step 3.
⏱ Time Allowed
3:00
Normal clock criteria: All numbers in correct sequence and approximately correct position (12, 3, 6, 9 in anchor positions). No missing or duplicate numbers. Hands point to 11 and 2 (representing 11:10). Hand length not scored.
🧠
Step 3 — Word Recall
Spontaneous recall, no cueing
3 of 3
Say to patient: "What were the three words I asked you to remember?"
Do not cue or prompt. Record words spontaneously recalled only.
Reminder: Only count words recalled spontaneously without any cueing from you.
